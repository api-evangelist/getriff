We weren’t surprised to hear that 600 odd people were less creative collaborating on Zoom as compared to in person, in Bruck’s & Levav’s paper published in Nature in April 2022 . Zoom wasn’t built for the remote era, nor was it built for teams to riff on ideas quickly and creatively. This new era of creative work within product teams calls for a new tool.
